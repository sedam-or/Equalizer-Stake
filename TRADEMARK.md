The project name and logo ("the Marks") identify the official project and the managed cloud. The **code** is free under AGPL-3.0, but the Marks are **not** licensed under AGPL.

**You may, without asking:**
- use the name to refer to the project accurately ("runs on <Name>", "a <Name> preset for swimming schools");
- use the unmodified logo in articles, tutorials and talks about the project;
- distribute unmodified official releases under the name.

**You may not, without written permission:**
- name a fork, a modified distribution or a hosted service with the Marks or anything confusingly similar;
- use the Marks in a way that suggests endorsement or partnership;
- use the Marks in a domain name, app-store listing or social handle.

Running a modified version? Rename it and say "based on <Name>". Questions: open a Discussion in the "Trademark" category.
