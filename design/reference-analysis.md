# BMD Music School — Reference Site Analysis & Synthesis

> Analisis 4 reference sites → sintesis untuk BMD Music School

---

## 1. Reference Sites Analyzed

### A. Yousician (yousician.com)
**Tipe:** Music learning app (global)

**What works:**
- **Hero:** Split layout (50/50) — text left, image right
- **Headline:** Short, punchy, benefit-focused ("Master Guitar in 6 Months")
- **CTA:** Bright green pill button ("Get Started"), high contrast
- **Instrument grid:** 5 instruments as clickable cards (direct to vertical)
- **Upsell block:** Purple highlight for "Family Plan" — different from main flow
- **Color:** White bg, black text, green accent only on CTAs
- **Typography:** Large bold sans-serif, high whitespace
- **Social proof:** Numbers (10M+ users, 500+ songs)

**What doesn't work for BMD:**
- Green color ≠ BMD brand (we use blue + gold)
- App-focused (we're physical location)
- Global audience (we're local Yogyakarta)

---

### B. Fender Play (fender.com/play)
**Tipe:** Guitar learning platform (global)

**What works:**
- **Hero:** Dark bg with high-quality lifestyle image
- **Headline:** Simple, emotional ("Start Playing Today")
- **CTA:** White outline on dark bg (contrast)
- **Lesson preview:** Shows actual lesson content (builds trust)
- **Progress visualization:** Shows student journey
- **Testimonials:** Video testimonials (higher trust than text)

**What doesn't work for BMD:**
- Dark theme (we use light)
- App/platform focused
- Global audience

---

### C. Masterclass (masterclass.com)
**Type:** Online courses from celebrities (global)

**What works:**
- **Hero:** Full-width cinematic video
- **Headline:** Premium feel ("Learn from the World's Best")
- **Instructor cards:** Celebrity photos + credentials
- **Grid layout:** Clean 3-column course cards
- **Social proof:** "Join 10M+ students"
- **Upsell:** "Get All Access" bundle pricing

**What doesn't work for BMD:**
- Celebrity-focused (we're local teachers)
- Premium pricing ($180/year) — different market
- Video-heavy (performance burden)

---

### D. General Music School Landing Pages

**Common patterns across local music schools:**

1. **Hero:** Photo of students playing + CTA
2. **Instruments:** Grid of instrument cards with images
3. **About:** Teacher photos + credentials
4. **Testimonials:** Parent quotes
5. **Pricing:** Simple tiers (trial, regular, premium)
6. **Contact:** WhatsApp + phone + address
7. **Gallery:** Studio photos (builds trust)

## 2. Synthesis: What BMD Should ADOPT

### From Yousician:
✅ **Split hero layout** — text left, image right (not full-width gradient)
✅ **Instrument grid as direct links** — click instrument → go to specific page
✅ **Bright CTA contrast** — one main CTA, stands out from everything else
✅ **Social proof numbers** — "150+ families" not "our school is great"

### From Fender Play:
✅ **Lesson preview** — show what a lesson looks like (builds trust)
✅ **Progress visualization** — show student journey (beginner → advanced)
✅ **Video testimonials** — higher trust than text quotes

### From Masterclass:
✅ **Clean grid layout** — 3-column cards with consistent height
✅ **Premium feel through whitespace** — less is more
✅ **Bundle pricing** — family discount / multiple instrument pricing

### From Local Music Schools:
✅ **WhatsApp as primary CTA** — Indonesian users prefer WhatsApp
✅ **Studio gallery** — photos build trust more than words
✅ **Teacher profiles** — names + credentials + photos

## 3. Synthesis: What BMD Should REMOVE

### From current BMD:
❌ **Full-width gradient hero** — too "app-like", not premium enough
❌ **ALL CAPS everywhere** — too aggressive, feels shouting
❌ **Generic "Kenapa BMD" section** — doesn't differentiate
❌ **Static testimonial cards** — no real photos, no video
❌ **No studio photos** — missing visual proof
❌ **No teacher profiles** — missing trust element
❌ **No student journey visualization** — no progress shown

### General:
❌ **Too many sections** — current 9 sections → consolidate to 7
❌ **No clear conversion path** — hero CTA should lead somewhere specific
❌ **No urgency** — no limited time offer, no scarcity

## 6. Color Recommendation

### Current palette:
- Primary: #2563EB (blue) — KEEP, but use less
- Accent: #F59E0B (gold) — KEEP for CTAs only
- Background: #F8FAFC — KEEP
- Text: #1E293B — KEEP

### Recommended addition:
- **Success green:** #10B981 — for "free trial" badge or "150+ families" highlight
- **Hero accent:** Consider warm gradient (gold → orange) instead of blue → purple
  - Reason: Warm colors = more emotional, better for music/education
  - Blue → purple = more tech/SaaS feel

### Color usage rules:
1. **Hero:** Warm gradient (gold → orange) OR keep blue but make it feel premium
2. **CTAs:** Gold #F59E0B (primary), Blue #2563EB (secondary)
3. **Cards:** White bg, no colored borders
4. **Text:** Dark gray #1E293B, never pure black
5. **Accents:** Use sparingly — one accent color per section max

## 7. Typography Recommendation

### Current:
- Headings: Plus Jakarta Sans (800 weight, uppercase)
- Body: Inter (400 weight)

### Recommended adjustment:
- **Headings:** Plus Jakarta Sans 700 (bold, NOT extrabold)
- **Hero headline:** Plus Jakarta Sans 800 (extra bold, for impact only)
- **Body:** Inter 400 (regular)
- **CTA buttons:** Inter 600 (semibold)

### Size scale:
- Hero headline: 3rem / 3.5rem (desktop) — NOT 5rem (too big)
- Section headline: 2.5rem
- Subheadline: 1.25rem
- Body: 1rem
- Small: 0.875rem
- Caption: 0.75rem

### Spacing:
- Section padding: 4rem vertical (not 8rem — too much whitespace)
- Card padding: 2rem
- Gap between sections: 2rem

## 9. Summary: BMD Design Direction

### The Vibe:
**"Premium tapi hangat. Profesional tapi ramah."**

### Visual Identity:
- Clean, airy, lots of whitespace
- Warm colors (gold, orange) balanced with blue
- Real photos (studio, teachers, students) — NOT emoji/icons
- Minimal animation (fade-in on scroll, smooth hover)

### Content Strategy:
- Benefit > Feature
- Emotional > Technical
- Numbers > Adjectives
- Transformation stories > Static quotes

### Conversion Path:
- Hero CTA → WhatsApp chat → Trial session → Register → Pay
- Every section should have ONE clear next step

### What Makes BMD Different:
1. **Local** — Yogyakarta, Seturan (not generic online course)
2. **Personal** — Guru tetap, jadwal fleksibel
3. **Proven** — 10+ tahun, 500+ alumni
4. **Holistic** — Bukan hanya musik, tapi karakter
