# Ritma brand guidelines

How to use the Ritma name, logo, colour, type and voice. **Strategy** (problem, ICP, positioning, USPs, naming) lives in [BRAND.md](BRAND.md). The **product UI** design system lives in [spec 09](../spec/09-design-system.md) and [spec 27](../spec/27-ux-states-and-feedback.md). This file covers marketing, the README, docs, social posts, slides and anything else that represents the project.

Inside the app, each school's own logo and brand hue lead (DS-03). These guidelines govern the Ritma brand around the product, not a school's branding inside it.

Use of the name and logo by others is governed by [TRADEMARK.md](../TRADEMARK.md).

## 1. Brand in one line

**Ritma: the rhythm of your school.** (*Irama sekolah Anda.*) Calm, plain, warm, proof over promises.

## 2. Logo

### 2.1 The mark

A circle divided like a bar of music in four beats. One beat is highlighted: one time slot taken, the others free. The mark reads as a clock, a rhythm and a calendar slot at the same time (BRAND.md §7).

Construction (64-unit grid, files in [`logo/`](logo/)):
- Outer radius 30, ring inner radius 20, gap between quarters 5 units.
- The **top-right quarter is a solid quarter disc** (the beat). The other three are ring segments (the rests).
- The beat is solid, not just a different colour, so the mark survives one-colour printing and 16 px.

### 2.2 The wordmark

Lowercase **ritma** in Plus Jakarta Sans 700 with −1% tracking, outlined to paths, so it renders the same without the font installed. Never retype the wordmark in a live font. Use the files.

### 2.3 Lockup

Mark on the left, wordmark on the right. The mark is 56 units high. The wordmark sits with its baseline at 51.5 units and starts 16 units after the mark (gap = 0.29 × mark height). This is the only lockup. There is no stacked version yet. Open an issue with the `brand` label if a layout needs one.

### 2.4 Files

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `logo/ritma-lockup.svg` | Default, on light backgrounds |
| `logo/ritma-lockup-dark.svg` | On dark backgrounds (gray-900/950, brand-900+) |
| `logo/ritma-lockup-mono.svg` | One colour, uses `currentColor` (inline SVG) or black (as an image) |
| `logo/ritma-mark.svg`, `-dark.svg`, `-mono.svg` | Avatars, app icons, small spaces, social profile |
| `logo/ritma-wordmark.svg`, `-dark.svg` | Only when the mark already appears nearby (e.g. a page header under a big mark) |
| `logo/favicon.svg` | 16 px grid version with thicker ring and 1.5-unit gaps; switches colours with `prefers-color-scheme` |
| `assets/banner-light.svg`, `assets/banner-dark.svg` | README header (`<picture>`), 1280×320 |
| `assets/social-preview.svg` + `.png` | GitHub social preview and link cards, 1280×640 (GitHub needs the PNG) |

All files are plain SVG: no scripts, no external references, no embedded fonts, no raster images. That is the same subset the tenant-logo sanitiser keeps (SEC-14).

### 2.5 Clear space and minimum size

- **Clear space:** at least ¼ of the mark height on every side (16 units on the 64 grid). Nothing else (text, edges, other logos) enters it.
- **Minimum size:** lockup 96 px wide on screen / 25 mm in print. Mark alone 16 px (use `favicon.svg` at 16–24 px, `ritma-mark.svg` from 24 px up).

### 2.6 Colour versions

| Background | Rests (ring) | Beat | Wordmark |
|---|---|---|---|
| Light (white, gray-50/100) | brand-600 `#005EB9` (6.36:1 on white) | accent-700 `#AA5F04` (4.83:1) | gray-900 `#14191F` |
| Dark (gray-900/950) | brand-400 `#4693F1` (6.29:1 on gray-950) | accent-400 `#F9AD26` (10.35:1) | gray-50 `#F9FAFC` |
| Photo or busy colour | Use mono white or mono gray-900 on a solid panel | — | — |

Every part of the logo keeps at least 3:1 against its background (WCAG 1.4.11), computed with the WCAG 2.x formula used in `scripts/palette.py`. The brighter accent-500/600 is **not** used for the beat on light backgrounds, because it drops below 3:1 on gray-50.

### 2.7 Misuse

Don't:
- recolour the mark outside §2.6, add gradients, shadows, outlines or 3D,
- rotate the mark (the beat is always top-right: the first beat of the bar),
- fill all four quarters, or highlight a different quarter,
- stretch, condense, or change the gap,
- retype the wordmark, capitalise it ("Ritma" in running text is fine; the logo is always lowercase),
- place the light version on dark backgrounds or on photos without a panel,
- combine the logo with a school's logo as if it were a joint brand (refer to it in text instead, e.g. "runs on Ritma", TRADEMARK.md).

## 3. Colour

The palette is the product palette. OKLCH in spec 09 §2.1 is normative. Hex values come from spec 09 §2.6 (generated by `scripts/palette.py`).

| Role | Token | OKLCH | Hex | Share of a layout |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Background | gray-50 / white | 0.985 0.003 255 | `#F9FAFC` / `#FFFFFF` | ~70% |
| Text | gray-900 | 0.210 0.015 255 | `#14191F` | ~15% |
| Secondary text | gray-600 | 0.450 0.020 255 | `#4E5661` | |
| Brand | brand-600 | 0.49 0.20 255 | `#005EB9` | ~10% (headlines accents, buttons, links, the mark) |
| Brand tint | brand-100 / brand-300 | 0.93 0.04 255 / 0.78 0.11 255 | `#D9EAFF` / `#87BAFD` | Fills, slot motif |
| Accent | accent-700 (light) / accent-400 (dark) | 0.56 0.13 60 / 0.80 0.16 75 | `#AA5F04` / `#F9AD26` | **≤ 5%**: the beat, one highlight per layout |
| Dark background | gray-950 | 0.150 0.012 255 | `#080B10` | Dark versions |

Rules:
- **One accent moment per layout.** The amber beat is the "slot taken" moment. Two amber elements cancel each other out.
- Text on colour follows the contrast table in spec 09 §2.6 (≥ 4.5:1 for text, ≥ 3:1 for graphics).
- Status colours (green, amber, red, blue, violet) are for product states, not for marketing decoration.

## 4. Typography

Both families are SIL OFL and self-hosted in the product (spec 09 §2.3).

| Style | Family | Weight | Size / line (px) | Tracking | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Display | Plus Jakarta Sans | 700 | 64/72 | −1% | Landing hero, slide titles |
| Headline | Plus Jakarta Sans | 700 | 40/48 | −0.5% | Section titles, banners |
| Title | Plus Jakarta Sans | 600 | 28/36 | 0 | Cards, social posts |
| Tagline | Plus Jakarta Sans | 500 | 40/48 (banner), 24/32 (inline) | −0.5% | "The rhythm of your school." |
| Body | Inter | 400 | 18/28 marketing, 16/24 docs | 0 | Paragraphs |
| Small | Inter | 500 | 14/20 | 0 | Captions, labels |
| Code | ui-monospace, JetBrains Mono | 400 | 14/22 | 0 | Commands, config |

- Sentence case everywhere, including headlines and buttons.
- Numbers in tables and prices use tabular figures.
- Maximum line length 72 characters for body text.

## 5. Layout and grid

- **Base unit 8 px** (the product's 4 px scale, doubled for marketing). Section spacing 96 px desktop, 64 px mobile.
- **12-column grid**, 1200 px max content width, 24 px gutters, 80 px outer margin on banners.
- Generous whitespace. One idea per section, one primary call to action per page.
- **The slot motif:** a small grid of rounded rectangles (radius 6) in brand-100/300 with exactly one accent cell, as in the banners. Use it as the only decorative element. It is a week of lessons with one slot booked. Don't animate it beyond a single fade-in.

## 6. Iconography, illustration, photography

- **Icons:** lucide, 1.5 px stroke at 24 px, round caps and joins, `currentColor`. Same set as the product (spec 24 OSS). No filled or duotone icon sets.
- **Illustration:** single-colour line drawings in brand-600 on brand-50, or brand-300 on gray-950. Same stroke weight as the icons. No characters with faces, no 3D, no blobs.
- **Photography** (BRAND.md §7, §9): real small-school moments, warm natural light, hands on instruments, a parent's phone with the schedule, a front desk. No stock "business people". **No children's faces without written parental consent.** No identifiable school or city without written permission.
- **Screenshots:** always real product UI with demo data (the seeded demo tenant), in a device frame, shown in light and dark pairs. Never mock data that contradicts the product (e.g. features that don't exist yet).

## 7. Motion

Marketing motion follows the product motion tokens (spec 09 §2.4): 120 / 200 / 320 ms, `cubic-bezier(.2, 0, 0, 1)` for enter. One motion per view, and it must explain something (the 3-step setup, a slot getting booked). Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. GIFs and videos have a static poster frame.

## 8. Voice and copy patterns

Voice and tone per audience are in BRAND.md §6. Patterns that apply to every surface:

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Set up your school in three steps." | "Revolutionise school management!" |
| "Two lessons can't book the same room. Postgres enforces it, and CI proves it." | "Enterprise-grade, AI-powered scheduling." |
| "Pre-release. Not ready for production yet." | Hiding the project's status |
| "Free to self-host. Cloud from Rp…/month." (when prices are set) | "Free forever*" with fine print |
| Numbers with sources ("saved 6 hours a week", with permission) | Invented statistics |

- Claims link to their proof (BRAND.md §4: a spec, a test, a badge).
- English and Indonesian versions say the same thing. Neither is a summary of the other.
- Banned words: revolutionary, AI-powered, enterprise-grade, disrupt, seamless, best-in-class.
- Indonesian uses *Anda* in product and docs, *Bapak/Ibu* in parent messages.

The source SVGs are the templates: copy one, change the text paths or swap them for live text in your editor, and export. To re-outline text in Plus Jakarta Sans, use any editor's "convert text to outlines". The font's OFL licence allows outlined artwork.

## 10. Checklist before publishing

- [ ] Logo from `brand/logo/`, unmodified, correct version for the background, clear space kept.
- [ ] One accent moment, contrast ≥ 4.5:1 for text and ≥ 3:1 for graphics.
- [ ] Every claim is true today and links to its proof, and pre-release status is visible.
- [ ] No children's faces without consent, no school or city named without permission.
- [ ] EN and ID versions match.
